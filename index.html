Put this on your home screen first

Do this before you set anything up. On iPhone, the home screen app keeps its own copy of your figures, separate from Safari.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari and open Money from your home screen.
  1. In Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.
  3. Open Money from your home screen.

            Your pay

            If your payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, the app moves it to the working day before, as the NHS does. If a month pays early (December often does), put that date in the one-off box. It only changes that month.
